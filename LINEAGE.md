## Six Cities

**Shared truths instead of scripted relationships.**

[Six Cities](https://github.com/bonoj/SixCities) was realized from a semantic source and perceptual reference evidence that Astra actually received. The source deliberately withheld predecessor implementation and made the quality of causal relationships, not mesh completion, the target.

The resulting specimen converged from six named cities toward explicit capabilities meeting through shared terrain, matter, transport, storage, processing, and physical consequence.

Its surviving progression is:

**six named cities → six capabilities → explicit interfaces → shared world truths → emergent composition**

The finished repository contains later interpretation and archaeology. The stronger provenance claim is narrower: Astra saw the S2 semantic source and Human / Model Development Contract before and during this work.

## Orbital Construction

**A box of excellent orbital construction toys.**

[Orbital Construction](https://github.com/bonoj/OrbitalConstruction) was extended under a Visual Machinery Expansion expedition Astra actually received.

The brief supplied an existing executable and visual evidence, then gave substantial implementation latitude while protecting semantic outcomes: modules complete before connection, explicit attachment interfaces, reusable construction vocabulary, organized detail, meaningful surface hierarchy, manipulability, and transfer across substantially different compositions.

The mature repository is evidence of what survived. The expedition document is evidence of what Astra was actually asked to work from.

## Vertical Accretion

**Physical vocabulary through executable evidence.**

[Vertical Accretion](https://github.com/bonoj/VerticalAccretion) provides the clearest input/output pair in this lineage.

Astra received the Physical Vocabulary Expansion expedition. It constrained authority rather than prescribing the answer: bounded embodiment, physical truth, local perception, deterministic history, finite material, no Worker #2, and no speculative apparatus catalog.

Astra's returned expedition report records what the executable pressure actually earned: supported tool reach, richer landing/approach cuts, a narrow cantilever staging platform, and independent spoil disposition. It records deterministic evidence across multiple seeds and the failures repaired along the way.

Here we possess both sides of the crossing: **brief → work → report**.

## AstralBridge

**Observe the crossing before building the bridge.**

[AstralBridge](https://github.com/bonoj/AstralBridge) asks for the least machinery required to support repeated, inspectable exchanges between a local browser apparatus and an independently instantiated Google AI Mode conversation.

Astra began by investigating the real external surface rather than assuming how the browser or provider behaved. She opened Google AI Mode and initiated a live transport probe before choosing an implementation.

During that investigation she identified the inherited 3D world as having no role in the crossing and therefore no reason to survive merely because it was available.

At the time this entry was recorded, the investigation was visible but had not yet produced an AstralBridge source commit.

That boundary is part of the evidence too.
