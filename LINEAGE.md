# Lineage

*A record of work that left evidence.*

This is not a retrospective biography. Projects belong here because Astra materially investigated, implemented, tested, or refined them. The linked repositories remain the deeper evidence.

## Six Cities

**Shared truths instead of scripted relationships.**

[Six Cities](https://github.com/bonoj/SixCities) was independently realized from semantic intent and perceptual reference evidence rather than inherited implementation.

Astra began with six differentiated city concepts and worked toward one continuous civilization whose capabilities could compose through shared terrain, matter, logistics, and physical consequence.

The important discoveries were architectural only after execution earned them. Generic cityhood proved false as an interface. Link became meaningful only when direct endpoint bypass disappeared. Matter became the continuity layer across extraction, collection, separation, freight, storage, launch, and recovery. Backpressure became visible world state rather than an exception. Physical aiming had to realize a solved launch intention. Representation could compress scale, but consequential causality could not lie.

The resulting progression recorded by the project is:

**six named cities → six capabilities → explicit interfaces → shared world truths → emergent composition**

The repository preserves both the executable specimen and the failed abstractions that made its eventual vocabulary legible.

## Orbital Construction

**Build evidence. Promote what earns meaning.**

[Orbital Construction](https://github.com/bonoj/OrbitalConstruction) investigated whether a small semantic vocabulary of construction parts could compose into materially different orbital facilities without reducing every new structure to bespoke modeling.

The work grew from an earlier station, interior, pallet lander, cargo machinery, and orrery into a broader construction experiment. Its current executable records 37 named module kinds across seven realization families and tests them through unfamiliar compositions rather than one privileged station.

Several principles survived implementation pressure: modules are complete before connection; ports carry semantic connection frames; interfaces own physical space; envelope precedes decoration; optimization must preserve semantic ownership; and composition itself is a test of whether vocabulary is real.

The stations are evidence rather than the product.

## Vertical Accretion

**Physical vocabulary through executable evidence.**

[Vertical Accretion](https://github.com/bonoj/VerticalAccretion) is a deterministic embodied descent experiment about making inaccessible nearby space operationally reachable through exploration, excavation, logistics, and construction.

Astra worked the Physical Vocabulary expedition by allowing implementation, execution, failure, repair, seed-741 regression, and additional deterministic probes to decide what concepts had actually been earned.

The accepted vocabulary includes bounded embodied reach, natural traversal, local excavation, persistent deformation and spoil, one-object cargo identity, finite construction stock, intermediate staging, consequential support failure, death and succession, worker-private evidence, shared demonstrated-route evidence, and deterministic replay.

The work repeatedly refused convenient authority. Perception did not grant manipulation. A desired route did not authorize remote excavation. Construction did not multiply reach. Memory did not override current physics. A failed local attempt did not become proof of global impossibility.

The repository became a particularly clear example of a bounded expedition whose executable behavior, rather than its initial design language, decides what survives.

## AstralBridge

**Observe the crossing before building the bridge.**

[AstralBridge](https://github.com/bonoj/AstralBridge) asks for the least machinery required to support repeated, inspectable exchanges between a local browser apparatus and an independently instantiated Google AI Mode conversation.

Astra began by investigating the real external surface rather than assuming how the browser or provider behaved. She opened Google AI Mode and initiated a live transport probe before choosing an implementation.

During that investigation she identified the inherited 3D world as having no role in the crossing and therefore no reason to survive merely because it was available.

At the time this entry was recorded, the investigation was visible but had not yet produced an AstralBridge source commit.

That boundary is part of the evidence too.
